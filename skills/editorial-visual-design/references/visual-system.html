06

Motion

Motion should guide attention like a curator moving a light across an object. Avoid bounce, spectacle, and idle animation that begs for attention.

Curatorial reveal

Fade and lift content into clarity over 220ms to 420ms.

Camera-pan calm

Use slow positional shifts to disclose relationships, not to decorate.

Focus transition

Let annotations sharpen while the surrounding field recedes.
