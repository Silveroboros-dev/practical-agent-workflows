# Style Cheat Sheet

## Essence

- dark editorial gallery
- treasure hunt, not funnel
- non-homogeneous composition
- netsuke energy: intricate craft revealed up close
- calm first read, hidden complexity on interaction

## Canonical Frame

Use `Direction / Keep / Avoid / Prompt language` as the default response shape.

## Impression Pattern

General pattern:

1. The audience arrives expecting a conventional version of the subject.
2. The surface feels calm, sparse, and highly composed.
3. Interaction or closer reading opens unexpected depth and structure.
4. The audience leaves sensing one authored intelligence behind the whole system.

Reference example, research archive:

A visitor expects a conventional document index, then discovers an annotated set of related ideas across disciplines.

## Rare Differentiators

- `Treasure hunt`, not funnel.
- `Gallery-like but non-homogeneous`, not a polished product landing page.
- `Netsuke energy`: intricate craft revealed at close range, not loud ornament.
- `Unexpected modality`: interaction logic should express the mind behind the work.
- `Interdisciplinary credibility`: domains should feel authored together, not stitched together.

If these five disappear, the style has drifted.

## Keep

- charcoal-black base
- soft white and muted gray type
- warm brass accent, used sparingly
- archival paper, matte metal, smoked glass, graphite, stone
- authored asymmetry
- medallions, sigils, and quiet circular markers
- thin borders, fine separators, restrained blur
- editorial hierarchy with generous whitespace

## Typography

- Headings: light humanist or quiet display sans.
- Body: contemporary grotesk, calm and highly readable.
- Labels: tiny uppercase whisper labels with restrained tracking.
- Good fallback families: Avenir Next, Geist, Inter, Sohne, Neue Haas Grotesk, Helvetica Neue, IBM Plex Sans.
- Use condensed or serif faces only with intent; avoid defaulting to fashion-magazine luxury.

## Luxury Rule

Good luxury means material restraint, exact spacing, tactile surfaces, and intellectual structure.

Bad luxury means glossy shorthand: banking-premium UI, fashion moodboard drama, gold-on-black excess, or expensive-looking surfaces without substance.

## Motion

- curatorial reveal
- camera-pan calm
- slight fades, lifts, and focus transitions
- surprise through unfolding structure, not visual tricks

## Avoid

- cyberpunk
- neon palettes
- purple-on-black AI cliches
- glossy "premium" banking UI
- startup gradient blobs
- gamer aesthetics
- generic AI swirls
- moodboard luxury without intellectual structure
- overpacked pages that reveal everything at once

## Image Rule

A series of images should feel like one publication mind, not a rotating moodboard.

## Good Subject Anchors

- annotated diagrams
- archival paper, notes, ledgers, schematics
- matrices, conceptual maps, trees, grids
- matte metal, stone, smoke, paper, glass
- quiet studies, galleries, libraries, institutional interiors
- artifacts that imply inquiry, systems, or interdisciplinary depth

## Short Prompt

> Dark editorial-gallery aesthetic with charcoal-black atmosphere, warm brass accents, archival paper and matte metal details, authored asymmetry, visible negative space, and hidden structure. Intellectual, restrained, tactile, and quietly authoritative. No cyberpunk, no startup gloss, no purple neon, no generic AI swirls.
